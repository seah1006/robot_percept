## r5 cell-only assets
- `assets/objects/cell_r5/*.obj`: 6 original procedural meshes authored for this project (metres), no third-party geometry/texture. Matte opaque colors. Mug/bottle cylinders are simplified surrogates, not exact product replicas.
- `cad_uploads/converted/box_fillet_50x30x20mm/` and `cup_d70_h60mm/`: project-original geometry generated by `tools/make_samples.py` using CadQuery and converted with `tools/stp_to_mesh.py` (baseline a408c2d). No third-party source geometry. Box 50x30x20mm fillet3mm; cup diameter70mm height60mm wall3mm.
- Mustard references the existing YCB CC BY 4.0 visual, texture and collision hull, unchanged.
